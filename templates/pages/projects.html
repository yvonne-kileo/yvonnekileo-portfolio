HOW I BUILD

Every project starts with a problem.

I enjoy building systems that have a practical purpose. Rather than focusing only on the code, I try to understand the problem first and then design a solution around it.

01

Understand

Identify the real problem, the people involved and what the system needs to accomplish.

02

Design

Break the problem down into features, data, interfaces and system workflows.

03

Develop

Build the system step by step while testing the functionality along the way.

04

Improve

Review what works, identify weaknesses and continue improving the solution.
